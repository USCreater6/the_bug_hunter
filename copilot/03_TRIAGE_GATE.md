# 03 — Triage Gate (run BEFORE submitting)

Answer every line honestly. One "no" = do not submit. This protects your Signal score.

- [ ] **Reproducible:** I reproduced it at least 3 times, from a clean state.
- [ ] **In scope:** the affected asset is explicitly in scope.
- [ ] **Not excluded:** the bug class is not on the program's exclusions / common non-qualifying list.
- [ ] **Real impact:** I can state concrete harm — who is affected, what data or action, and why it matters. Not theoretical.
- [ ] **Not inflated:** the severity I claim matches the impact I actually demonstrated.
- [ ] **Not a visible duplicate:** I checked disclosed reports and the policy's known-issues list; it's not there. (Private duplicates can't be ruled out — accept that risk.)
- [ ] **Evidence clean:** request/response + screenshots/video attached, all cookies/tokens/PII redacted.
- [ ] **Own accounts only:** every repro used my own test accounts and synthetic data.
- [ ] **No chaining beyond need:** I stopped at the minimum needed to show impact; I did not pivot into other users' data.

If all boxes are ticked: it's eligible to submit. The decision to submit is still **yours** — Claude never submits.

If any box fails: shelve it, or go back to the hunt loop and strengthen the weak step.
